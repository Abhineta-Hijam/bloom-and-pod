This page explains how we handle information collected through Bloom & Pod order and customer-support services. For order terms, please also see our Terms & Conditions.

1. Introduction

Bloom & Pod respects the privacy of customers and visitors using our website. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how and why we use it, how we protect it, and how customers can contact us about their information.

By placing an order or submitting information through our website, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy. Where consent is required by applicable law, we will request it separately.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information provided directly by customers when placing an order, checking order status, requesting cancellation, return, refund or replacement, or contacting support. Depending on the service requested, this may include:

  • Name and contact details, such as email address and phone number.
  • Shipping address, PIN code and delivery instructions.
  • Order ID, purchased products, quantities, order status and delivery details.
  • Information used to verify an order or cancellation request, including the registered phone number.
  • Messages, reasons for cancellation, grievance details and supporting photographs or videos voluntarily submitted for a claim.
  • Payment status and transaction references needed to verify or reconcile an order; we do not ask customers to send payment passwords, PINs or one-time passwords to support.

We ask customers to provide only information reasonably necessary for the relevant service. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive personal details in support messages or attachments.

3. How We Use Your Information

Customer information is used for purposes connected with operating Bloom & Pod, including:

  • Confirming orders and verifying customer identity or ownership of an order.
  • Processing purchases, payment confirmations and refunds when applicable.
  • Preparing, shipping and delivering ordered products, including communicating delivery updates.
  • Providing order tracking, cancellation and other customer-service functions.
  • Investigating and resolving complaints, damaged-item claims, returns, replacements and other customer grievances.
  • Maintaining accurate transaction records, preventing misuse or fraud, and meeting applicable legal obligations.

We do not use customer information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis or the consent required by applicable law.

4. Advertising and Promotional Communications

Bloom & Pod may use contact information to send product updates, offers, announcements or other advertising and promotional communications where the customer has opted in or where such contact is otherwise permitted by applicable law.

Promotional communications are separate from essential transactional messages such as order confirmations, verification messages and delivery updates. Customers may request to stop promotional communications at any time by using the opt-out instructions in a message, where available, or by contacting us using the details in Section 11. Opting out of promotions will not prevent communications needed to fulfil an active order or address a service request.

5. Cookies and Website Tracking

Bloom & Pod does not intentionally use cookies for advertising or behavioural tracking. Certain browser storage technologies may be used for essential website functionality, including retaining shopping cart information.

Some browsers, hosting platforms, payment services or other technical providers may use their own essential storage or similar technologies when their services are accessed. These are separate from cookies intentionally deployed by Bloom & Pod. If our use of cookies or tracking technologies changes, we will update this policy and provide any notices or choices required by law.

6. Information Sharing and Service Providers

Bloom & Pod does not sell, rent or share customer information with unrelated third parties for their independent advertising or marketing.

To complete a customer's requested service, limited information may need to be made available to those helping us operate the store—for example, delivery or courier partners require a recipient's name, address and contact details to deliver a parcel; payment processors may handle payment information; and website, database or hosting providers may process information needed to run the site. We limit such access to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant service and require appropriate safeguards where applicable.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, a valid legal process, or to protect against fraud or security threats in accordance with applicable law. We do not otherwise disclose information for unrelated third-party use.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational precautions to protect personal information against unauthorised access, misuse, alteration and loss. Access to customer details should be limited to authorised personnel and service providers who need that information to perform their duties.

No website, network or electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Customers should keep their Order ID and account or verification information private and should not share one-time passwords or sensitive payment credentials.

8. Data Retention

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to complete orders, support delivery, resolve grievances, maintain transaction records and meet applicable legal, tax or accounting requirements. Information that is no longer needed for these purposes will be deleted, anonymised or securely retained only where legally required or otherwise permitted.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and applicable obligations. We will review customer requests concerning deletion in light of those requirements.

9. Customer Choices and Privacy Requests

Subject to applicable law, customers may contact us to request access to or correction of their personal information, raise a privacy concern, withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing, request deletion of information that is no longer required, or opt out of promotional communications.

To protect customer information, we may verify that a request comes from the relevant customer before acting on it. Some records may need to be retained to complete a transaction, resolve a dispute or comply with law. We will respond to requests and grievances within the timeframe required by applicable law.

10. External Services and Policy Updates

Our website may link to external websites or services, such as payment or delivery providers. Their independent privacy practices are governed by their own notices, and customers should review those notices when using those services.

We may update this Privacy Policy when our operations, data practices or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page, with an effective date when appropriate. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
