Refund, Return and Replacement Policy

We are committed to delivering healthy plants, fresh flowers, saplings, heirloom seeds, pods and other gardening products in good condition. Since many products are living, organic or perishable, this policy explains how we assess delivery issues, returns, refunds and replacements fairly and transparently. For order cancellations, please see our Cancellation Policy.

1. Refund and Replacement Request Window

Customers are generally required to initiate a refund or replacement request within 48 hours from the recorded delivery timestamp.

The delivery timestamp is recorded in the order timeline and can be viewed on the Order Status page. The 48-hour window begins as soon as the order status is updated to Delivered.

How to submit a request

  1. Visit the Order Status page on our website.
  2. Enter your Order ID and registered email address.
  3. Click Check Order Status.
  4. Select the appropriate option from the dropdown menu: Initiate Refund/Replacement/Return.
  5. Submit your request with the required details and supporting evidence.

The online request option will close when the 48-hour window expires. Customers are encouraged to inspect their orders promptly and report any issues as soon as possible.

This standard request window does not limit any consumer rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded or restricted.

2. Unboxing Video and Evidence Requirements

As our products may be perishable or susceptible to transportation damage, customers are strongly advised to record a clear, continuous 15–20 second unboxing video when opening their parcel.

For faster assessment, we recommend submitting the video within 24 hours of the recorded delivery timestamp.

Please email your request and supporting evidence to:

Email: example@example.com

Use one of the following email subject lines:

  • Request for Refund – Order [Order ID]
  • Request for Replacement – Order [Order ID]
  • Request for Return – Order [Order ID]

Please include the following information in your email:

  • Order ID and registered email address
  • A brief description of the issue
  • The unboxing video in a viewable format, preferably MP4
  • Additional photographs, if necessary

The video should clearly show the parcel, the products received and any visible damage.

Our team will review the available evidence and communicate the outcome to your registered email address. Additional information may be requested when reasonably necessary.

An unboxing video helps us verify transit damage and improve our packaging and delivery processes. However, the absence of a video will not automatically invalidate an otherwise legitimate claim or any rights available under applicable law.

3. Product-Specific Refund and Replacement Policy

For general product categories, see Section 2.1 of our Terms & Conditions. We distinguish perishable and living goods; non-perishable goods; and seeds, dried botanical products and other organic materials that need product-specific assessment. Classification alone does not determine claim eligibility.

3.1 Plants and Saplings

A refund or replacement may be considered if a plant or sapling arrives dead or severely damaged during transportation.

The general 20% damage assessment described in Section 4 will ordinarily apply to bulk transit-damage claims. Individual cases involving a completely unusable product, a material defect or other circumstances requiring separate assessment will be reviewed accordingly.

Natural variations in plant size, shape, leaf appearance and growth are expected, provided the product is otherwise healthy and consistent with its description.

3.2 Flowers

A refund or replacement may be considered if flowers arrive wilted, spoiled or damaged during transportation.

The extent of damage will generally be assessed against the total product value of the order, as explained in Section 4.

Minor natural variations in colour, bloom size and appearance will not ordinarily qualify as damage where the flowers remain in the condition reasonably described for the product.

3.3 Heirloom Seeds and Organic Pods

Heirloom seeds and organic pods are assessed as organic materials with product-specific storage, shelf-life and condition considerations; they are not automatically deemed perishable solely because they are organic.

A refund or replacement may be considered if these products arrive damaged or defective. Claims involving physical damage will generally follow the assessment method in Section 4.

Claims concerning incorrect products, missing quantities or material discrepancies from the product description will be assessed separately.

3.4 Packaged Seeds

Packaged seeds are assessed separately from heirloom seeds, taking into account packaging integrity, expected shelf life, storage conditions and product description.

If packaged seeds arrive damaged, defective, improperly sealed or materially different from their description, customers may request an appropriate resolution.

Returns of unopened packaged seeds may be considered under the conditions applicable to non-perishable products, subject to applicable law and any disclosed product-specific restrictions.

3.5 Wrong Product or Incorrect Quantity

If a customer receives a product different from the one ordered, or the delivered quantity is less than the quantity purchased, the customer may request a replacement or refund for the affected product or quantity.

These claims will be assessed separately and are not subject to the general 20% physical-damage threshold.

Please provide the Order ID and clear evidence of the product or quantity received.

3.6 Change of Mind

We generally do not offer change-of-mind refunds or replacements for perishable products after delivery.

This restriction is subject to applicable consumer protection laws and any rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded.

3.7 Plants That Die After Delivery

Plants and saplings are living products whose health depends on factors such as watering, sunlight, temperature, handling and environmental conditions.

A plant that arrives healthy but dies several days after delivery is generally not eligible for a refund or replacement under our standard transit-damage policy.

However, claims involving a latent defect, inaccurate product description, an applicable product guarantee or other circumstances covered by law will be considered separately.

Customers are encouraged to follow the care instructions provided with their products.

3.8 Non-Perishable Products

Non-perishable products, such as gardening tools, pots, planters, accessories and suitable sealed plant-care products, may be eligible for return, refund or replacement depending on the reason for the request and the condition of the product. Some organic materials and packaged goods require product-specific assessment as described in our Terms & Conditions.

Where applicable, returned products should be unused, complete and in their original packaging. Return eligibility and any applicable conditions will be communicated to the customer and applied in accordance with applicable law.

4. General 20% Damage Assessment Rule

For eligible perishable products, our general damage assessment considers the value of the affected products across the entire order rather than assessing each item independently.

The damage percentage is calculated as follows:

Damage Percentage = (Value of Affected Products ÷ Total Product Value of the Order) × 100

For this calculation:

  • The total product value is based on the actual prices paid for the products after applicable product discounts.
  • Shipping charges and other non-product fees are excluded.
  • The value of affected products is based on the portion of the order verified as damaged or unusable.
  • For partially damaged products, the assessed affected value will reflect the extent and nature of the damage rather than automatically treating the entire product as damaged.

How the threshold is applied

Damage exceeding 20%: A refund or replacement may be considered for the affected products or an appropriate portion of the order, based on the evidence and circumstances.

Damage of 20% or less: The claim will ordinarily be assessed individually. Depending on the severity of the damage, product usability and evidence available, we may offer a partial refund, replacement or another suitable resolution.

The 20% threshold is a general bulk-assessment guideline. It does not automatically disqualify claims involving a completely unusable item, wrong products, missing quantities, material defects or other circumstances requiring separate consideration.

Nothing in this section limits any consumer rights or remedies available under applicable law.

5. Refund Policy

Where a refund is approved, it will be processed through the applicable payment method or another legally permitted method agreed upon with the customer.

The time required for the refund to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment provider, bank or payment method.

For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, customers may be asked to provide the information reasonably necessary to process the refund securely.

Where only part of an order is affected, the refund may be limited to the affected product or quantity, as appropriate to the circumstances.

6. Replacement Policy

Where a replacement is approved, we will arrange dispatch subject to product availability and delivery feasibility.

If the same product is unavailable, we may discuss an alternative resolution with the customer, such as a refund or another mutually acceptable option.

For perishable products, we generally do not require customers to ship damaged or spoiled items back when a return shipment is unnecessary.

7. Return Shipping and Product Handling

Returns of non-perishable products must follow the return instructions provided by our support team.

Customers should not ship products back without first obtaining return instructions from us.

For perishable products, return shipping will generally not be required for verified transit damage or spoilage when photographic, video or other evidence is sufficient for assessment.

Any return or shipping conditions will be applied in accordance with applicable consumer protection laws.

8. Claim Assessment and Communication

Each claim will be assessed based on the available evidence, product category, order details, extent of damage and applicable policy conditions.

We may request additional information where reasonably necessary to understand the issue or investigate suspected fraudulent claims.

Our decision and any approved resolution will be communicated to the customer's registered email address.

We aim to handle claims fairly, consistently and within a reasonable period.

9. General Conditions

  • Customers are encouraged to inspect their deliveries promptly, especially when orders contain living or perishable products.
  • Customers should follow the care, watering, sunlight and handling instructions provided with their products.
  • Claims should include accurate order details and genuine, unaltered supporting evidence.
  • Natural variations in living products are not automatically considered defects where the products remain consistent with their descriptions.
  • We may investigate suspected fraudulent or misleading claims.
  • Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude or restrict consumer rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded or restricted.
