2.1 Product Classification: Perishable and Non-Perishable Goods

For the purposes of our store policies, Bloom & Pod generally groups products into the following three categories. This classification guides handling and claim assessment; it does not, by itself, determine eligibility for a refund, return or replacement.

A. Perishable and Living Products

  • Live plants, saplings and live plant cuttings.
  • Fresh flowers and fresh floral arrangements.
  • Other fresh or living botanical products that may deteriorate or change quickly without appropriate care.

These products require suitable handling, storage and care. Their return, refund and replacement eligibility is subject to the Refund & Return Policy and applicable law.

B. Non-Perishable Products

  • Gardening tools, pots, planters, containers and accessories.
  • Sealed or packaged plant-care products designed for storage.
  • Suitable packaged organic manure, compost and vermicompost, when their labeling and storage requirements support longer-term storage.
  • Other durable gardening supplies.

These products may be considered for return, refund or replacement under the applicable product-specific conditions and relevant law.

C. Seeds, Dried Botanical Products and Other Organic Materials

  • Heirloom seeds and commercially packaged seeds.
  • Organic pods, dried flowers and dried botanical materials.
  • Other naturally derived goods whose shelf life or storage requirements differ by product.

These items are assessed according to their condition, packaging, expected shelf life, storage needs and product-specific characteristics. They are not automatically classified as perishable merely because they are organic or naturally derived.

Important: Claims involving damaged, defective, incorrect, missing or materially misdescribed products are considered under the Refund & Return Policy and applicable consumer protection laws. Product classification alone does not automatically approve or exclude any claim.
