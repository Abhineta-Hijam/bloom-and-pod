Cancellation Policy

This policy explains when Bloom & Pod orders can be cancelled and the steps customers must complete to verify and submit a cancellation through our Track Order page.

1. When Cancellation Is Available

Customers may cancel their order while its current status is New or Processing, or within 4 hours of placing the order, whichever is earlier. Cancellation is not available through our standard customer cancellation system once the order status changes to Packed. Delivered or already-cancelled orders cannot be cancelled again.

Cancellation eligibility is determined using the order's current status when the cancellation request is processed. An order may become ineligible if it advances to shipping before cancellation is confirmed.

2. How to Cancel Your Order

  1. Open the Track Order page.
  2. Enter your Order ID and the email address registered with your order.
  3. Select Check Order Status to view the order details.
  4. For an eligible order, select Cancel Order.
  5. Verify the registered phone number and provide a reason for cancellation.
  6. Review your details and select Yes, Cancel Order to confirm.

Once cancellation succeeds, the tracking page will show the order as Cancelled along with its cancellation details.

3. Registered Phone Number Verification

To help prevent unauthorised cancellations, customers must verify the 10-digit phone number registered with the order. Enter the registered number into the verification field and select Verify Phone Number.

Verification must be successful before the cancellation can be submitted. A successful verification is valid for approximately five minutes and is intended for a single cancellation submission. If verification expires, customers must verify their phone number again.

Incorrect verification details or repeated unsuccessful verification attempts may temporarily limit further attempts to protect customer orders. Phone verification is an additional check, not a substitute for protecting access to your order details.

4. Cancellation Reason

Customers must provide a reason for cancellation. The reason field is mandatory and accepts up to 200 characters. Please describe the reason briefly and accurately so it can be recorded against the order.

5. Confirmation and Effect of Cancellation

When an eligible cancellation is successfully confirmed, the order status changes to Cancelled. The cancellation date, time and submitted reason are recorded with the order. A successful cancellation cannot be reversed through the customer tracking page.

An unsuccessful verification or cancellation request does not cancel the order. Customers should check the order's status on the tracking page before assuming a cancellation has been completed.

6. Shipped and Delivered Orders

Once an order is shipped, it cannot be cancelled through the standard cancellation process. After delivery, customers may seek a return, refund or replacement where eligible under our Refund & Return Policy.

A return, refund or replacement request is a separate process from cancellation and is subject to the applicable policy conditions and consumer rights.

7. Administrator and Supplier Rights

The Administrator and/or Supplier reserves the right to reject, suspend, or cancel any order, either before or after order confirmation, where reasonably necessary due to product unavailability, pricing or listing errors, payment verification issues, suspected fraudulent activity, failure to meet applicable terms and conditions, or other legitimate operational reasons.

Where an order is cancelled, the customer shall be notified as soon as reasonably practicable. Any refund due shall be processed in accordance with the applicable Refund Policy and prevailing laws of India.

8. Assistance and Consumer Rights

If you are unable to access your order or need help with a cancellation, please contact customer support at example@example.com.

Nothing in this policy excludes or limits any consumer rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded or restricted.
